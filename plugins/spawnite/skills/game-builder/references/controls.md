# Controls

Ask: is this played on a keyboard, on a phone, or both? Recommend what the prompt names. When it names nothing, recommend the keyboard, unless the games under **Inspiration** are played on a phone. WASD and the arrow keys walk the character; a phone needs the on-screen controls in `engine/ui/joystick` and `engine/ui/tap`.

Tell the player the controls on the lobby's screen, in the words for the device: "WASD walks" on a keyboard, "drag to walk" on a phone.

Prove it: a screenshot of the lobby with the controls, and, for a phone, one of a round with the on-screen controls showing.
